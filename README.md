# app.fae.academy

This app will be part of my Advanced Technician's Final Project in Web Application Development. App Fae Academy is a whimsical, cozy and productive videogame.

The theme is inspired from the book Fae Academy: The Pursuit of Cheng (Fae Academy: La Búsqueda de Cheng in it's original language) by Paola Fucheng. It's also inspired from Stardew Valley, Animal Crossing, Fae Farm and Spirit City: Lofi Sessions when it comes as a videogame. To be a web application I got inspired in Lofi Town and Club Penguin. And at least, as a productive app is inspired in Forest and To Do List MinimaList.

To consolidate the previous paragraph, the map and the characters users can create, are inspired in the book. And all the apps mentioned will help create the dynamics and controls, furthermore in the productivity part there will be a minimalist Pomodoro or Timer and a To Do List.

As an artist, I need to hightlight that AI will not be used for the art creation, such as: map, characters, music, et cetera. As a programmer, I'll reduce the usage of AI and search in Internet, webs or forums, or not use it at all. Only AI has being shown in the search of Google with Gemini, but not used. AI has being shown when searching in Internet or Pinterest with images already made by other people, too.

The videogame will be in English and Spanish, and maybe later in French. All process documentation is in English due programming is mostly in English and I'm more comfortable in this languague.

### Story of the videogame
The videogame will start far away from Fae Academy, with Fourky as protagonist and the only player character available until the user will have enough FAE coins to have the passport, known as the FAE letter, given to only a few special people by this institution.

Fourky is a little frog that knows all kinds of magic and is the head teacher of the Amis. He lives alone in his tree house surrounded by nature and his little garden. Here he can do his chores without worrying about anything in his cozy corner.
